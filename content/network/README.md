# I Promise the Route Is Good

Under everything you do online there is machinery that decides where your traffic goes, and most of it works by taking a
stranger's word for it. A router hears where something now lives, and passes the news along. Nobody signs anything.
Nobody checks.

You play the one telling the lie, quietly, from the edge, and the unsettling part is how ordinary everything stays while
you do. The packets still arrive. They simply arrive by way of you first, and every map still reports that the traffic
reached where it was sent. It did not.

The game turns on the trade that runs through the whole business: a quiet vantage you can hold for weeks, set against a
louder move that gives you more and is seen by everyone the instant you make it. It is the fastest way to feel why "
nothing looks wrong" and "nothing is wrong" are not the same sentence.

A world in words, played through a cursor. 

    ./play network
