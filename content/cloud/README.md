# I Promise I Belong Here

Somebody's password turned up in an old leak and was never changed. That is the whole of the way in. No lock is broken
and no alarm sounds, because from the inside you look exactly like someone who is allowed to be there, which, as far as
anything can tell, you now are.

What it shows you is the quietest kind of loss: the post read for months while every dashboard reports a calm and
blameless year. You play it as a patient operation built from small, unremarkable steps, each of which the system
performs exactly as designed. The temptation is always to reach for more. The discipline is knowing that the reach is
the one thing anyone would ever see.

It is worth an unhurried half hour for anyone who has ever taken comfort from a green dashboard. Afterwards the green
means something a little different.

A world in words, played through a cursor.
