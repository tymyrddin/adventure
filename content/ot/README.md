# I Promise I'm a Qualified Engineer

A laptop is open in an empty office after hours, still warm, not yet locked. The sticker on the lid names the contractor
who looks after the water treatment works on the edge of town.

You are the person who sat down at it. Everything after that is a sequence of small decisions, what to read and what to
touch and how far to go, and the game's whole argument is that you should feel each one. A patient move can open a way
you did not know was there. A single loud one can shut the quiet route for good and leave you back where you started,
having learned exactly what it cost.

Nothing here is broken into and nothing is blown up. That is rather the point. The most consequential moves in a place
like this are the ones that look like nothing at all, and the quickest way to understand what the people guarding it are
up against is to spend twenty unhurried minutes on the other side of the desk.

It is a world in words, played through a cursor.
