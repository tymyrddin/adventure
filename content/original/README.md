# The Old Working

There is a hole in the floor of a round room on a hillside, with iron rings set round the edge of it, and somebody once
meant to come back for whatever went down there. They did not.

This is an adventure in the old way: a place made entirely of words and explored through a blinking cursor. You go
in with a brass lamp and a short shovel and whatever else you can carry, and you find out what the hill is by trying
things and reading what comes back. Some ways are shut until you have done the thing that opens them. One or two are
shut the moment you do.

It is small, and meant to be finished in a single sitting, the way the games it descends from were. It asks for a little
patience and a willingness to type `look` one more time than feels dignified.

To go in:

    ./play original
