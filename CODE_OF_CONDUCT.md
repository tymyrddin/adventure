# Code of conduct

The worlds here are full of people behaving badly towards one another on purpose. That is the
fiction. The place where the actual people are runs the other way: assume the other person is
trying, read them generously, and be the company you would have wanted one room behind you
when you were stuck.

## In practice

* Argue with the idea, never the person. "That world has a dead end the solver misses" is
  welcome; "you are an idiot" is not.
* Be patient with a first question you answered for yourself years ago, give credit, and take
  correction as well as you hand it out.
* Keep the games in the games. Adversarial play belongs inside a `world.toml`, not in an
  issue, a thread, or anyone's inbox.

Harassment is not tolerated in any of its shapes: insults and slurs, demeaning people, unwanted attention that carries 
on after a clear no, threats, or publishing someone's private details. None of it is excused by being framed as a joke 
or "just how I talk". This holds everywhere the project does, and binds the people with the commit bit more firmly, not 
less.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainers privately through https://tymyrddin.dev/contact/, with what happened,
where, and when. You will be believed as a matter of course and kept out of the retelling as
far as we can manage. We act on every report, from a quiet word to being shown the door for
good, and we explain our reasoning to the people who need it.
